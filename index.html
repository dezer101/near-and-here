The small print,
in plain English.

This concept site keeps its terms and privacy notes easy to find. No fine-print maze required.

01 Terms of use

Near & Here is a portfolio concept that shares general neighbourhood inspiration. Place descriptions are illustrative, can change over time and should not be treated as real-time business information. Please check details with a venue before you visit.

By browsing this demonstration, you agree to use it lawfully and understand that the content is provided for general information. The site is supplied as-is for learning and demonstration purposes.

02 Privacy note

This static demo does not ask for your name, precise location or account details. Search runs in your browser; the search text is not sent to a server by this page. This project does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

If the concept were developed into a real service, its privacy notice would need to describe any new data collection before it was introduced.

03 Accessibility and feedback

The page is designed to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings and small screens. If you find something difficult to use, contact the project owner through the portfolio repository where you found this demo.

✳ This is a learning project. Its sample places and policy text are illustrative, not legal advice or a live directory.
